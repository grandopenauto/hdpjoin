Common questions

Before you get started

Can you build for iPhone and Android?

We can scope iOS and Android projects, including cross-platform approaches. The build, testing, signing and app-store release plan depends on the required features and development environment. We'll confirm feasibility and any Apple or Google account requirements before agreeing to delivery.

Do I need a complete specification?

No. A description of the problem and intended users is enough to begin. We can help break it into a bounded first release with clear milestones.

Who owns the final application?

Source code, designs, credentials, third-party licenses and any reusable HDP components are addressed in the written agreement before development. We won't assume ownership terms on either side.

Will you quote a fixed price?

Once the essential requirements and dependencies are clear, we can discuss a scoped proposal and milestones. There is no universal fixed price for every app.

Share the goal, constraints and budget you have in mind. We'll use that context to discuss a realistic scope and next step—without assuming you need a large engagement.
