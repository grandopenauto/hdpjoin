Pilot eligibility

A small founding cohort, by design.

We're starting with a manageable group rather than collecting hundreds of signups for an unfinished platform. Educational, business, technology, maker, creative and commerce-focused creators are all reasonable fits.

Is joining free?

Requesting an invitation and using public HDPJoin tools are free. Any creator studio, community or hosted-store access is subject to an individually reviewed pilot scope. A possible no-cost UltraHype store does not imply free unlimited hosting, paid integrations, shipping, payment-processing fees or future terms.

Can I instantly launch a store or creator profile?

Not yet. UltraHype storefront provisioning, Social Network OS community access and teleprompter studio hosting require verified integration and operating policies. We'll describe the exact pilot capabilities before approval.

Do I have to promote HDP?

No. The point is to collaborate on content and tools that genuinely help your audience. Sharing links is optional; paid or incentivized referrals would require separate written terms and clear disclosures.

Who owns what I create?

You retain rights to your original work, subject to any existing third-party rights and platform terms. A pilot agreement would specify any permissions needed for display, collaboration or redistribution before anything is published.

Will I earn referral commissions?

Not automatically. This invitation page does not establish an affiliate program, commission rate, revenue share or payout entitlement. Such arrangements require specific approved terms, attribution and verified purchases.
