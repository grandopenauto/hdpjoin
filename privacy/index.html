Privacy & updates

Clear choices, including whether to measure your visit.

This site is a public informational website. It does not provide customer account registration or automated checkout.

What HDPJoin collects

This static site can be delivered through GitHub Pages, which necessarily handles basic server requests and may log technical access information under its own policies. HDPJoin does not run an account database, nor does it send your responses to the on-page discovery guide to a server.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics is disabled on HDPJoin unless you choose “Allow analytics.” If allowed, it can record page views and navigation events using the existing Highest Degree Priorities measurement property. You can change your preference using “Analytics preferences” in the footer. A local browser preference stores your selection; declining does not stop GitHub's basic hosting operations.

Email update requests

The “Request email updates” link opens your own email program addressed to contact@highestdegreepriorities.com. Sending that email shares your address and the content you choose to send with HDP through email. It does not automatically enroll you in a newsletter. HDP should confirm subscriptions and provide unsubscribe instructions before running an email campaign. You can request no further updates through the same contact address.

Videos and other websites

Links to Highest Degree Priorities, external specialist websites and demo systems leave HDPJoin and may have their own privacy terms. Future approved YouTube embeds are intended to load only after a viewer clicks to play, using the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com embed URL. No video embed is preloaded by default.

Cookies and local preferences

This site's own preference flag is stored locally in your browser, under the key hdpjoin.analytics-consent.v1. If you clear browser storage, you may see the analytics choice again. Optional Google Analytics may set cookies after you allow measurement.

Questions or removal requests

Email contact@highestdegreepriorities.com for privacy concerns, information requests or to stop receiving updates.
